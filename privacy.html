Arth Book - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Arth Book is a business record-keeping app (customers, traders, items, bills) for commission agents (arhtis) in fruit, vegetable and grain markets. This page explains what data the app uses.

Data stored on your phone

Your business records are stored on your own device. We do not have access to them unless you turn on a cloud package (see below).

Google sign-in and Google Drive backup

If you choose to back up to Google Drive, the app asks for Google permission using the drive.file scope only. This lets the app create and read only the backup files that the app itself created in your Google Drive. The app cannot see your other Drive files. Your Google email address is used only to remember which account you selected on your device. Backup files stay in your own Google Drive; they are not sent to us or to any third party. You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access).

Optional cloud package (employees)

If the shop owner buys a package that includes employees, shop data is synced to a secured Google Firebase database so the owner and employees can use the same records. Access is limited to that shop. Packages without employees do not use cloud data.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your business records with anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete the app data from your phone, and delete backup files from your Google Drive at any time. To request deletion of any cloud data, contact us at the email below.
